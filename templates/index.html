Smart Suggestions & Safety Guidelines

💰

Never Pay Registration Fees

Legitimate employers will never ask for money for interviews, training kits, or equipment setup.

📧

Check Domain & Email Format

Be cautious if recruiters use free domains like @gmail.com instead of official corporate emails.

💬

Beware of Off-Platform Chat

If interviews are strictly on Telegram or WhatsApp without video calls, proceed with extreme caution.

🔍

Verify Company Existence

Cross-check the company on LinkedIn and Glassdoor to confirm active employees and physical address.
